How to play

  • Tap the right answer before the timer bar runs out to hit the monster.
  • Answer in the first half of the bar (the gold part) for a critical hit: double damage.
  • Wrong answer or too slow: the monster hits you and you lose a heart. Lose all 5 and it gets away.
  • Combo: every 5 right in a row adds +25% damage.
  • Win a fight to rank up. Each rank makes the timer shorter, gives you more damage and brings a tougher monster.
  • On a keyboard, press 1–4 to answer (or ← → when there are two choices).
